Session Pet

A small pixel critter lives in your menu bar and reflects your live Claude Code (tmux) sessions — momentary, not persistent. Click below to try the pin and approve/deny mechanics.

The four states

Idle

No active session. Calm, steady — no motion.

Working

Claude is thinking or running a tool. Brighter green, focused eyes.

Waiting

Needs your approval. Amber, wide eyes, slow glow pulse.

Error

Session errored. Dim, desaturated, sad eyes — no motion.
